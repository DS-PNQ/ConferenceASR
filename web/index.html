Hy-MT2 FP8 translation

Speaker diarization

Volume-based turn detection out of the box; swap to NVIDIA NeMo MSD/Sortformer via one config flag.

CUDA + CPU fallback

bf16 on your RTX 4060, float32 on CPU, automatic OOM retry — plus a demo mode with zero weights.

DeepFilterNet denoising

Full-band voice suppression runs before ASR — room noise and fans stripped out, speech kept. Toggleable per session, CUDA-accelerated.
